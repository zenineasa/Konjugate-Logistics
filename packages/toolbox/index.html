        4 Model

        5 Scenarios

        Each scenario forks the model on the day it starts and runs beside the baseline.

        Fewer ships through a strait or canal for a while: the ports that depend on it receive less.

        A road lane closed, or restricted, for a while. Trucks already on it finish their trips.

        Fewer trucks (drivers short, trucks off the road) or more (the operator hires). Trucks are hired or released over a few days; only idle ones are released.

        Towns order more for a while, as on peak days.
